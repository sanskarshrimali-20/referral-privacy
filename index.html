Privacy Policy

Referral - Connect & Refer Application

Last Updated: October 2023

Welcome to Referral ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application and related services.

By downloading, accessing, or using the Referral application, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not access or use the application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering on the application, submitting or requesting job referrals, or interacting with our services.

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, mobile number, company name, designation, professional skills, and profile picture.
  • Job Referral Details: Job positions, company names, job descriptions, domain categories, and referral credits.
  • Resumes & Uploaded Documents: PDF resumes uploaded by applicants to apply for job referrals.
  • Proof Screenshots: Verification images uploaded by referrers to demonstrate referral submissions.
  • Payment & Payout Information: UPI ID and transaction/withdrawal history for credit purchases and earnings payouts.
  • Device & Usage Data: Secure authentication tokens, system log errors, and usage statistics to improve app functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for specific operational and service-related purposes:

  • To create and maintain your user account.
  • To enable job seekers to request job referrals from verified referrers.
  • To share applicant resumes with referrers for evaluation and application processing.
  • To process credit purchases and UPI withdrawal payouts accurately.
  • To send push notifications and status updates regarding your referral requests (e.g., Pending, Resume Seen, Accepted, Referred, Rejected).
  • To prevent fraudulent activities, guarantee platform safety, and enforce our terms of service.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and only share data under the following circumstances:

  • With Referrers: When an applicant requests a referral, their profile and uploaded PDF resume are shared with the relevant referrer.
  • Service Providers: We use Supabase as our secure backend service provider for authentication, cloud database, and document storage.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or court order.
  • No Commercial Sale: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data or resumes to third-party advertisers.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal data:

  • All network communications are encrypted in transit via SSL/HTTPS.
  • Resumes and profile images are stored securely in encrypted cloud storage buckets.
  • Sensitive local data (such as login tokens and UPI credentials) is saved using device-level secure encryption storage.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your personal information:

  • Access and Edit: You can view and update your profile information anytime through the "Edit Profile" screen in the app.
  • Account Deletion: You can request full deletion of your account and associated data by contacting our support team.
  • Permissions: You can control notification and storage permissions directly from your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
